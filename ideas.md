# LumaHire design direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Editorial Signal
Very clean job discovery with an editorial feel: warm paper tones, confident ink typography, and restrained indigo accents. The product feels human, considered, and premium rather than corporate.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Quiet Orbit
A calm, spacious interface built around pale blue-gray surfaces, soft shadows, and small moments of motion. The mood is reassuring and organized for candidates navigating change.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Electric Ladder
A sharper product language with dark ink panels, vivid indigo signals, and kinetic gradients that make searching feel energetic. Use only where momentum and contrast add clarity.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Editorial Signal

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial SaaS, borrowing from independent magazines and premium productivity tools.

**Core Principles:** Make the next action obvious; use whitespace as structure; make data feel warm and legible; reserve color for momentum and trust.

**Color Philosophy:** Warm ivory keeps the experience human and approachable. Deep ink navy creates confidence and strong text contrast. Electric indigo marks action and possibility, while a small lavender accent adds optimism without turning the interface into a gradient-heavy template.

**Layout Paradigm:** An asymmetric, left-anchored composition with a wide narrative hero and a floating search module that cuts across the hero/content boundary. Content then resolves into editorial rails: featured roles, compact category chips, and company signals.

**Signature Elements:** A horizon-and-spark brand mark; hairline path motifs inspired by career trajectories; pill-shaped indigo action cues paired with square-ish paper cards.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Every interaction should reduce uncertainty. Search, save, filter, and apply states should confirm themselves quickly with a small movement, color shift, or toast. Navigation remains direct and never traps the user.

**Animation:** Use brief 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, cards, and menus. Stagger hero and card entrances lightly. Avoid excessive parallax; let the hero texture sit quietly behind the content. Respect reduced motion preferences.

**Typography System:** Fraunces for display headlines and editorial emphasis; Plus Jakarta Sans for body copy, labels, and controls. Headlines are large and slightly tight; UI text is compact with generous line-height.

**Brand Essence:** LumaHire helps thoughtful candidates find work that fits their life, not just a list of openings. Personality: clear, optimistic, discerning.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are direct and hopeful; CTAs are specific and active; microcopy is warm, never salesy. Example lines: “Make your next move count.” “Save the roles worth a second look.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A compact horizon arc meeting a rising spark, paired with a custom-spaced LumaHire wordmark. The mark should remain recognizable at small sizes without relying on the full wordmark.

**Signature Brand Color:** Signal Indigo `#5B5CE2` — bright enough to guide action, calm enough to feel trustworthy.
